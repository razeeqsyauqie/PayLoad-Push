Square card (hand and deck)

Same parts as the Push card, squeezed into a square: Power hexagon, role badge, name band, and a short ability. Heroes that can move get an orange arrow badge under the role.

  1. Power: green when buffed, red when lowered
  2. Role: Tank, Damage or Support
  3. Move badge: only on heroes whose ability moves them
  4. Name band
  5. Ability: one line, max about 8 words

On the board

Once placed, a card becomes a token: art and Power only, with a thick blue frame for you or red for the enemy. When a hero is flipped, the frame changes colour with a white flash.
