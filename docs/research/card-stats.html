Payload Push card stats

All 57 cards, from 30,000 simulated Hand of 3 games with the three preset decks and 100,000 games with random legal custom decks. Click any column to sort.

What stands out

Torbjörn and Juno lead

Torbjörn decks win 54%, Juno decks 53.5%. Juno now buffs only the deck, which barely changed her: the Reserve part was small.

Soldier, Orisa, Pharah, Reaper

The next tier, at 52–53%. Mauga at 6 Power fell to about average (49%), and Emre at 3 to about 52%.

D.Mon Mech, Tracer, Anran are weakest

Decks with them win 47%. D.Mon fights at 6 without Baby D.Mon, and the AI almost never plays Baby D.Mon first.

Biggest synergy swings

Sojourn (4% to 95%), D.Va Mech, D.Mon Mech, Ashe, Ramattra, Freja and Widowmaker are weak alone and very strong once set up.

Hazard is underplayed

He wins 81% of the battles he is in, but the AI plays him only 11% of the time he is in hand, so decks with him only win 48%.

Roadhog and Kiriko now average

At 10 Power Roadhog wins 71% of his battles even after paying for his hook. Kiriko at 6 is played twice as often. Both sit at 50%.

How to read it

Play rate is how often the AI plays the card when it has it in hand.
Win rate is battles won when the card is played, in real games.
Vacuum is a one-on-one against every hero with nothing set up: no Reserve, no partners, Payload in the middle.
Synergy is the same one-on-one with the card's best set-up, named under the number.
Deck impact is how often a random custom deck containing the card wins the game. 50% is average.
Tier comes from deck impact, because it also counts after-battle abilities that win later rounds.

Simulated with the game's own AI, so play rate reflects how the AI chooses cards and a human may play some cards better. Vacuum and synergy only count the battle itself, so cards whose value comes after the battle (Mercy, Juno, Zenyatta, Brigitte, Lúcio) look low there. Numbers are from the current build: Ramattra 3, Juno 5, Kiriko 6, Roadhog 10, Torbjörn 7, Brigitte and Jetpack Cat swapped, Mauga 6, Emre 3, Juno deck only, Hazard 8 Power with −1 per Damage hero from both sides, Anran discards the strongest Reserve card, Tracer 4 with hand +2 recall. Baby D.Mon is never played by the AI, so its win rate is blank.
