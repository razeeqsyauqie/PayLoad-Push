How to play

Pick a mode to read its rules.

  1. Draw

    Fill your hand back up to 3 hero cards. The enemy does the same.

  2. Play 1

    Pick one hero to play. Cards that stay in your hand for a round become your Reserve.

  3. Reveal

    Both heroes flip face up at the same time.

  4. Abilities

    Each hero’s ability fires. Some raise Power, some lower the enemy’s, some bend the rules. Unblockable abilities go through barriers. Heroes with no ability simply have more Power.

  5. Compare Power

    Higher Power wins the fight. Equal Power is a draw.

  6. Push

    The winner moves the robot 1 step toward the enemy base.

Your Reserve is in your hand

  • A card becomes a Reserve card once it has stayed in your hand for a round. It gets a blue “Reserve” tag. A card you draw and play straight away was never in your Reserve.
  • When an ability names “your Reserve card”, it means your strongest Reserve card.
  • Many heroes get stronger when a partner waits in your Reserve, or once a partner has been played. A glowing “Synergy” tag shows when a bonus is ready.
  • Some abilities reach into the Reserve: they can buff, steal, swap or discard the card waiting there. Both Reserves are face up.
  • Supports help other heroes. Many power up the next hero you play, shown by a green tag on your hero slot. Tanks control the fight and the robot.
  • When your deck runs out, the heroes you played shuffle back in. Cards discarded by abilities stay out.

Keywords

  • Reserve: a card that has stayed in your hand for a round.
  • Next: waits for the next heroes you play, then wears off.
  • After battle: happens once the battle is over.
  • Unblockable: goes through shields, barriers and hacks.
  • Hack: cancels a hero’s ability for this battle, or longer if the card says so. Power from other cards stays.
  • Debuff: anything that lowers a hero’s Power.

Quick Play mode

  • Draw 1 card each round. Play it, or save it in your single Reserve slot.
  • If you reserve your new card, you play the top card of your deck instead, sight unseen.
  • On a later round, play either your new card or your Reserve card.
  • Pick Push, Control, Quick Play or Mystery Hero on the mode screen after you tap Play.

Control mode

  • No robot. The match is best of 3 rounds: win 2 rounds to win.
  • Each round lasts up to 5 turns. Win 3 turns and the round ends early in your favour.
  • After 5 turns, whoever won more turns takes the round. An even score is a drawn round.
  • There are 3 rounds at most. If nobody has won 2 rounds after round 3, the match is a draw.
  • It plays like Push: 3 cards in hand, play 1 each turn.

Mystery Hero mode

  • No deck to pick. You and the enemy each draw from your own shuffled pile of every hero.
  • You never draw the same hero twice, but the enemy can draw the same heroes you do.
  • It plays like Push: 3 cards in hand, play 1 each round.
  • The match lasts up to 20 rounds.

Winning

  • The robot starts in the middle.
  • Push it all the way to the enemy base to win.
  • If the enemy pushes it to your base, you lose.
  • Right-click a card (or press and hold on a phone) to inspect it and see its buffs and debuffs.
  • After 15 rounds (20 in Mystery Hero), whoever has the robot on the other side wins. If it’s in the middle, it’s a draw.

  1. Pick

    Choose a hero from your hand and a green tile. The bot does the same in secret.

  2. Reveal

    Both heroes land at once. If you picked the same tile, the higher Power takes it.

  3. Flip

    Damage heroes flip a weaker enemy next to them. Tanks guard the heroes around them. Supports boost a flipped hero next to them by +2, and win it back if that beats the enemies around it.

  4. Build

    Heroes stay all match. Make lines of 3 or more and grab bonus tiles.

  5. Score

    After 4 turns, add up your heroes' Power plus bonuses. Winning a round helps your next hero.

  6. Win

    After round 3, the higher board score wins the match.

Mayhem rules

  • A board 7 wide with a 5-tile back row on each side. The glowing centre tile scores double, and green bonus tiles give +2 or +3 to whoever holds them. 3 rounds, 4 turns each.
  • Each turn you and your opponent both pick a hero and a tile in secret, then reveal at the same time.
  • You can never place on the enemy's back row. When you have no heroes on the board (like the first turn), you can place anywhere else, except the centre tile on the very first turn. Otherwise you can always use your back row, and each role has its own spots: Tanks go next to any hero (not diagonally), Supports next to any hero (diagonals too), and Damage heroes anywhere in a row or column with one of your heroes. If you both pick the same tile, the higher Power takes it. A tie knocks both out.
  • Heroes stay on the board for the whole match. Each role does something different when it lands. Damage flips one weaker enemy next to it (+2 when attacking). Tanks don't flip, but they and your heroes next to them get +2 when defending. Supports don't flip either: they take back one of your heroes next to them that the enemy flipped. Heroes from two rounds ago are locked and can't be flipped.
  • Lines: 3 or more of your heroes in a straight row or column score a bonus (3 = +3, 4 = +5, 5 = +8, longer lines more).
  • Abilities can be turned off on the deck screen (Power only, the current test). With them on, many heroes buff allies in their row, column or next to them, or weaken nearby enemies.
  • Every round ends with a score: each hero you own scores its Power, and the hero on the centre tile scores double. Winning a round gives your first hero next round +2 (+4 after two wins). A hero played while your score is lower gets +2.
  • Heroes with the orange arrow can move. On your turn, before your new hero lands, tap one of them on the board and pick an orange tile. One move per turn, and heroes from two rounds ago are locked and can't move.
  • The match goes to the higher board score at the end of round 3.
  • Your deck has 15 heroes. You start with 10 random ones and the other 5 join your hand at round 2. You play 12, so 3 are left over.
